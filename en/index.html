About us

Close to home, looking out at the world.

Headway began in 1996 with a simple idea: to support people and companies in their relationship with languages. Today we work through two complementary specialisms: in-company training with Grupo Headway Training and courses abroad with Headway Language Services.

We are a small, close-knit team. We listen, design and support; we don't sell off-the-shelf packages. That is how we have worked for almost three decades.
